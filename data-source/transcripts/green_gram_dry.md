## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_measures.png`)

1. **Cercospora brown spot**:
   - `Use recommended spacing`
   - `Maintain field and crop sanitation`
2. **Powdery mildew**:
   - *(No printed text under photo in chart)*
3. **Anthracnose brown spot**:
   - `Use healthy seeds`
   - `Crop rotation`
4. **Mung bean mosaic virus**:
   - `Use tolerant varieties -MI 6`
   - `Remove infected plants`
5. **Bruchid**:
   - `Legume weevil`
   - `Grains should be well dried- before storage`
   - `Grains should be mixed with ash`
6. **Collar rot**:
   - `Remove infected plant debris with soil`
   - `Improve drainage`
   - `Avoid irrigation from infected fields`
7. **Pod sucking bug**:
   - `Destroying of infected pods and plants debris`
   - `Recommended Insecticides`:
     - `Cabaril 85% WG 20g/10 ltr`
8. **Bean fly**:
   - `Establishment of cultivation in the- field in April/May since the damage is high in Yala season`
   - `Seed Treatment`:
     - `Thiomethocsam 70% WS`
   - `Recommended Insecticides`:
     - `Apply when appearing first pair of leaves`
     - `Diacinon 50 EC`
     - `Cabosulfan S`
9. **Leaf miner**:
   - `Spray water to wash leaf surface`
   - `Recommended Insecticides`:
     - `Abamectin 1.8 EC 6ml/10 ltr`
