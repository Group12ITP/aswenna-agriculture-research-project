## Tile 05: Controlling (Pest & Disease Management) (`05_pest_control_all.png`)

### Thrips
- Chemical Controls:
  - `Thiacloprid 24 g / l SC`: `10 ml /10 ltr`
  - `Imidacloprid 20 g / l SL`: `10 ml /10 ltr`
  - `Fipronil 50 g / l SC`: `10 ml /10 ltr`
  - `Prothiophos 50 g / l EC`: `30 ml /10 ltr`

### Anthracnose & Purple blotch
- Chemical Controls:
  - `Pyraclostrobin 250 g/l EC`: `08 ml /10 ltr`
  - `Chlorothalonil 500 g/l SC`: `250 ml /10 ltr`
- Cultural / Agronomic Measures:
  - `Use of diseases free bulbs`
  - `Maintain sanitation`
  - `Crop Rotation`

### Damping-off
- Chemical Controls:
  - `Captan 50 g / l WP`: `06 g / ltr`
  - `Thiram 80 g / l SC`: `20 g / ltr`

### Fungal bulb rot & Bacteria bulb rot
- Seed Treatment:
  - `Thiophanate-methyl 70 g / l WP`: `20 gm / 10 ltr`
  - `Thiram 80 g / l WP`: `15 gm /10 ltr`
- Cultural Controls:
  - `Remove infected parts`
  - `Improve drainage facilities`
  - `Avoid irrigation from infestes cultivations` *(spelled "infestes")*

### Big onion caterpillar
- Pests pictured: `Spodoptera litura`, `Spodoptera exigua`
- Chemical Controls:
  - `Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG`: `4 mg / 10 ltr`
  - `Metaflumizone 24 g/l SC`: `25 ml / 10 ltr`
