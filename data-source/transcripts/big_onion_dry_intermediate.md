## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_measures.png`)

1. **Thrips**
   - `Thiacloprid 24 g/l SC 10 ml /10 ltr`
   - `Imidacloprid 20 g/l SL 10 ml /10 ltr`
   - `Fipronil 50 g/l SC 10 ml /10 ltr`
   - `Prothiophos 50 g/l EC 30 ml /10 ltr`

2. **Big onion caterpillar** (`Spodoptera litura`, `Spodoptera exigua`)
   - `Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG 4 mg / 10 ltr`
   - `Metaflumizone 24 g / l SC 25 ml / 10 ltr`

3. **Anthracnose**
   - `Crop rotation`
   - `Use recommended fertilizers`

4. **Purple blotch**
   - `Use diseases free bulbs`
   - `Maintain field sanitation`
   - `Crop Rotation`
   - `Remove infected parts`
   - `Use recommended fertilizers`

5. **Fungal neck rot** & **Fungal Bulb rot**
   - `Remove infected parts`
   - `Improve drainage facilities`
   - `Avoid irrigation from infected fields`

6. **Bacteria bulb rot**
   - `Use recommended fertilizers`

7. **Downy mildew**
   - *(Photo illustration included)*

### Characteristics of mother bulbs
- `Medium size bulbs`
- `Well matured bulbs`
- `Bulbs without mechanical damage`
- `Sealed bulb neck`
- `500 bulbs are required to produce seeds for an acre`

### Vernalisation
- `Store under 10 °C - 15°C for two weeks`
- `Suitable areas Riverston, Sita-Eliya & Rahangala`

## Tile 08: Nursery Management, Spacing, Bulb Treatments (`08_nursery_spacing_bulb_treatment.png`)

### Nursery management
- `3 m x 75 cm x 15 cm`
- `60-70 beds /acre`

### Seed requirement
- `2 1/2 - 3 kg /ac`

### Bed Sterilization
- `Burning of nursery bed`
- `Application of chemicals`
- `Polythene cover is used to protect from direct sun light. polythene cover should be at 3ft high.`

### Spacing
- **True seed production**:
  - `22.5 cm x 22.5 cm`
  - `22.5 cm x 15 cm`
- **Crop**:
  - `7.5 cm x 8 cm`
  - `10 cm x 10 cm`

### Bulb treatments
- `Thiophanate-methyl 70% WP`
- `Thiran 80% WP`
- `Thaiophanate-methyl 50% + Thiram 30% WP`
