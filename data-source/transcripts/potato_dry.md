## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_all.png`, `05a_pest_control_p1.png`, `05b_pest_control_p2.png`)

### 1. Late blight / Early blight / Stem blight
- **General Control**:
  - `For all blight conditions`
  - `Use of resistant varieties`
  - `Correct time of planting`
  - `Maintain a vigorous cultivation`
- **Recommended fungicides**:
  - `Chlorothalonil 500g/ltr SC`
  - `Mancozeb 75% WP`
  - `Maneb 80% WP`
  - `Propaneb 70% WP`
  - `Coper Oxicloride 50% WP`
  - `Cymoxanil 4.8% + Metharam 67%`

### 2. Leaf eating caterpillar
- `Destroy host plants`
- `Timely planting`

### 3. Aphids
- `Field inspection`
- **Recommended insecticides**:
  - `Imidecloprid 70% WG`
  - `Abamactin 18g/ltr ECT`
  - `Thioeyclam 50% SP`

### 4. Mites
- **Recommended insecticides**:
  - `Abamactin 18g/ltr IEC`
  - `Imidacloprid 200g/ltr SL`
  - `Sulphur 80%/WP/WG`
  - `Thiocyclam 50% SP`
