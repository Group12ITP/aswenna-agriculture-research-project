## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05a_pest_control_p1.png`, `05b_pest_control_p2.png`)

1. **Stem borer**:
   - `Remove and destroy crop residues - immediately after harvesting`
   - `Diazinon 5% GR 10 - 15 kg/ha`
   - `Fipronil 0.3% GR 12 kg`
2. **Pod borer**:
   - `Spay top side of the leaves at 25- 35 days and at 45-55 days`
   - `Novaluron 100 g /ltr EC 10 ml / 10 ltr`
   - `Etofenprox 100 g /ltr EC 15 ml / 10 ltr`
   - `Thiodicarb 375 g /ltr SC 20 ml /10 ltr`
3. **Aphids**:
   - `Recommended Insecticides`
   - `Imidacloprid 20 % SL 10 ml / 10 ltr`
   - `Imidacloprid 70 % WG 3 g /10 ltr`
   - `Thiodicarb 375 g /ltr SC`
4. **Penicillium seedling blight**:
   - `Storage`
   - `Seed moisture 13%`
   - `Seed treatment`
5. **Brown leaf spot, Sheath rot blight, Erwinia stalk rot disease, Rust**:
   - `Improving drainage`
   - `Mulching`
   - `Destroy infected crops debris` *(printed as debris instead of residues)*
   - `Crop rotation`
