## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_all.png`)

### Leaf webber
- Cultural Controls:
  - `Destroy leaf larvae during early stages of the damage`
- Chemical Controls:
  - `Tebufenozide 20 SC`: `15 ml / 10 ltr`
  - `Chlorfluazuron 52 EC`: `30 ml / 10 ltr`
  - `Neem extract`: `400 gm / 10 ltr`

### Mites & Tomato bug
- Cultural Controls:
  - `Avoid continuous cultivation on the same field`
  - `Proper attention is important`
  - `Destroy infected plant parts`

### Phyllody
- Agronomic / Vector Control:
  - `Plant hoppers act as vectors.`
  - `Control plant hoppers`

### Stem and foot rot
- Cultural / Agronomic Controls:
  - `Crop rotation`
  - `Improve drainage`
  - `Deep ploughing and expose to sunlight`
  - `Remove infected plants and plant parts`
  - `Avoid irrigation from infected fields`

### Soil Fertility Advice (Upper Right Box)
- `To improve soil fertility,`
- `Control soil erosion`
- `Use Gliricidia loppings to improve soil fertilitiy` *(verbatim spelling: "fertilitiy")*

#### Arithmetic Audit Note on Economics:
- Gross profit with family labour: 70,000.00 - 14,800.00 = 55,200.00 (Exact match).
- Gross profit without family labour: 70,000.00 - 32,400.00 = 37,600.00. The printed poster displays `7600.00`, omitting the leading digit '3'.
- Expenditure without family labour arithmetic: 900 + 250 + 500 + 24,000 = 25,650; the printed total of 32,400 reflects additional unitemized field expenditures (diff = 6,750).
- Expenditure with family labour arithmetic: 14,800 - 6,400 (labour) = 8,400 (total non-labour inputs).
