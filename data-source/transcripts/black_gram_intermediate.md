## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_measures.png`)

1. **Anthracnose**
   - `Use healthy seeds`
   - `Remove infected plants`
   - `Recommended fungicides`:
     - `Mancozeb 80% WP 20g/ 10 ltr`
     - `Chlorathalonil 500g SC 30ml/10 ltr`

2. **Collar rot**
   - `Remove infected plant debris - with soil`
   - `Improve drainage`
   - `Recommended soil fungicides`:
     - `Thiram 80% WP 70g/ 50 ltr/10m²`
     - `Thiophanate Methyl 70% WP`
     - `Thiram 30% WP 30g/ 50 ltr/10m²`

3. **Cercospora leaf spot**
   - `Use recommended spacing`
   - `Field and crop sanitation`
   - `Recommended fungicides`:
     - `Mancozeb 80% WG 20g/10 ltr`
     - `Propineb 70% WP 20g/10 ltr`

4. **Mosaic virus**
   - `Use tolerant varieties`
   - `Remove infected plants`
   - `Crop rotation`
   - `Control white fly`

5. **Bruchids**
   - `Legume weevil`
   - `Grains should be well dried before storage`
   - `Grains should be mixed with ash`
   - `Primiphos methyl 500g/ltr EC`

6. **Pod borer**
   - `Collect and destroy Pod borer`
   - `Remove and destroy crop residues`
   - `Recommended insecticides`:
     - `Thiodicarb 375g SC 10ml/10 ltr`
     - `Novaluron 100g EC 10ml/10 ltr`

7. **Bean fly**
   - `Early planting during Yala (April - May)`
   - `Seed treatment`: `Thiamethoxam 70% WS`
   - `Apply insecticides when first pair of leaves appear after planting`:
     - `Diazinon 500g/ltr EC /EW`
     - `Carbosulfan 200g/ltr SC`

8. **Leaf miner**
   - `Destroy crop debris`
   - `Use sticky traps`
   - `Apply neem extract`
   - `Spray water to wash leaf surface`
   - `Recommended insecticides`:
     - `Abamectin 1.8g EC 6ml/10 ltr`
     - `Cyromazine 75% WG 30g/10 ltr`
