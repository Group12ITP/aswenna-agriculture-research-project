## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_all.png`)

### 1. Late blight
- `For Late blight conditions`
- `Use resistant varieties`
- `Correct time of planting`
- `Maintain a vigorous cultivation`

### 2. Early blight
- `Wide spread during dry weather condition`
- **Recommended fungicides**:
  - `Propaneb 70% WP`
  - `Maneb 80% WP`
  - `Metholaxil 8% + Mancozeb 64%`
  - `Chlorothalonil 75% WP`
  - `Mancozeb 80% WP`
  - `Copper 50% WP`
  - `Propotocam 607g/ltr SL`

### 3. Black leg disease
- `Remove infected plants`
- `Improve drainage system`

### 4. Bacterial wilt
- `Use healthy seeds`
- `Crop rotation (Except other crops belong to Solanaceae family)`

### 5. Cut worm
- **Recommended insecticides**:
  - `Profenofos 500g/ltr EC`
  - `Prothiyofos 500g/ltr EC`

### 6. Aphid
- `Field inspection`
- **Recommended insecticides**:
  - `Imidecloprid 70% WG`
  - `Abamectin 18g/ltr ECT`
  - `Thioeyclam 50% SP`

### 7. Leaf miner
- `Use yellow coloured sticky traps`
- `Destroy host plants`
- `Introduce the parasite`

  - `Size - 28-55 mm`
  - `Having more than 5-6 active suckers of 2-3 cm in height`

  - `Spacing between rows:`
