## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_measures.png`)

- **Stem borer**:
  - `Novaluron 10 EC 10 ml / 10 ltr`
  - `Etofenprox 10 EC 20 ml / 10 ltr`
  - `Thiodicarb 375 SC 20 ml / 10 ltr`
  - `Crop rotation and removal of damaged plants`
  - `Ploughing immediately after harvesting`
  - `Apply recommended Nitrogen fertilizer levels`
- **Aphid**:
  - `Destroy crop reduces after harvesting`
  - `Recommended insecticides`:
    - `Imidacloprid 20 SL 10ml/ltr`
    - `Imidacloprid 70% WG 1.25g/10 ltr`
    - `Thiamethoxam 25% WG 2g/10 ltr`
- **Blast**:
  - `Use resistant varieties - "Oshadi"`
  - `Use quality seeds`
  - `Apply recommended spacing`
  - `Apply recommended fertilizer`
  - `Remove infected plants and plant debris`
