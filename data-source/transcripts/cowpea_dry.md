## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_measures.png`)

- **Collar rot**:
  - `Remove infected plant debris with soil`
  - `Recommended soil fungicides`:
    - `Thiram 80% WP 70g 50 ltr 10m²`
    - `Thiophanate Methyl 70% WP`
    - `Thiram 30% WP 30g/50 ltr 10m²`
- **Cercospora leaf spot**:
  - `Use recommended spacing`
  - `Field and crop sanitation`
  - `Recommended fungicides`:
    - `Mancozeb 80% WG 20g/10 ltr`
    - `Propineb 70% WP 20g/10 ltr`
- **Anthracnose**:
  - `Use healthy seeds`
  - `Remove infected plants`
  - `Recommended fungicides`:
    - `Mancozeb 80% WP 20g/10 ltr`
    - `Chlorothalonil 500g SC 30ml/10 ltr`
- **Mosaic virus**:
  - `Use tolerant varieties`
  - `Remove infected plants`
  - `Crop rotation`
  - `Control white fly`
- **Plant hopper**:
  - `Destroy host plants`
  - `Use sticky traps`
  - `Use recommended insecticides`
- **Blister beetle**:
  - `Collect and destroy beetles`
- **Aphid**:
  - `Vector of Mosaic virus`
  - `Timely cultivation`
  - `Application of recommended fertilizer`
  - `Recommended insecticides`:
    - `Imidacloprid 200 g/ltr SL 10ml/10 ltr`
    - `Imidacloprid 70% WG 10.25ml/10 ltr`
- **Leaf miner**:
  - `Destroy crop debris`
  - `Use sticky traps`
  - `Apply neem extract`
  - `Spray water to wash leaf-surface`
  - `Recommended insecticides`:
    - `Abamectin 1.8g EC 6ml/10 ltr`
    - `Cyromazine 75% WG 30g/10 ltr`
- **Pod borer**:
  - `Collect and destroy Pod borers`
  - `Remove and destroy crop residues`
  - `Recommended insecticides`:
    - `Thiodicarb 375g SC 10ml/10 ltr`
    - `Novaluron 100g EC 10ml/10 ltr`

## Tile 08: Risk Advice (`08_risk_advice.png`)

### DROUGHT
- `Use drought resistant varieties`
- `Use short duration varieties`
- `Mulching`

### HEAVY RAIN OR FLOOD
- `Improve drainage system`
- `Cultivate on ridges`
- `Use long duration varieties for Maha season`
- `Use short duration varieties to cultivate after rainy period`
