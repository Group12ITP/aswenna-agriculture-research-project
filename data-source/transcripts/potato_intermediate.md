## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_all.png`, `05a_pest_control_p1.png`, `05b_pest_control_p2.png`)

### 1. Late blight
- `For Late blight conditions`
- `Use resistant varieties`
- `Correct time of planting`
- `Maintain a vigorous cultivation`
- **Recommended fungicides**:
  - `Propaneb 70% WP`
  - `Maneb 80% WP`
  - `Metholaxil 8% + Mancozeb 64%`
  - `Propotocam 607g/ltr SL`

### 2. Early blight
- `Wide spread during dry weather conditions`
- **Recommended fungicides**:
  - `Chlorothalonil 75% WP`
  - `Mancozeb 80% WP`
  - `Copper 50% WP`

### 3. Stem blight
- *(Image illustrated; control grouped with blight recommendations)*

### 4. Bacterial wilt
- `Use healthy seeds`
- `Crop rotation (Except other crops belong to Solanaceae family)`

### 5. Cut worm
- **Recommended insecticides**:
  - `Profenofos 500g/ltr EC`
  - `Prothiyofos 500g/ltr EC`

### 6. Leaf eating caterpillar
- `Destroy host plants`
- `Timely planting`

### 7. Aphids
- `Field inspection`
- **Recommended insecticides**:
  - `Imidecloprid 70% WG`
  - `Abamactin 18g/ltr ECT`
  - `Thioeyclam 50% SP`

### 8. Mites
- **Recommended insecticides**:
  - `Abamactin 18g/ltr IEC`
  - `Imidacloprid 200 g/ltr SL`
  - `Sulphur 80%/WP/WG`

### Varieties & Seed Potato Specifications
- **Varieties**:
  - `Desiree (Red)`
  - `Granola`
  - `Rudolph (Red)`
  - `Arnova`
  - `Redlasoda (Red)`
  - `Condor`
- **Seed Potato Requirement kg/ac**:
  - `1000 kg/ac`
  - `(2500 kg/ha)`
- **Characteristics of Seed Potato**:
  - `Size - 28-55 mm`
  - `Having more than 5-6 active suckers of 2-3 cm in height`
- **Spacing & Field Amendments**:
  - `Spacing between rows:`
    - `for consumption: 60 x 25 cm`
    - `For seed potato: 45 x 25 cm`
  - `for liming - 2 tone/ha (to maintain 5-5.3 pH)`
  - `Organic fertilizer: 10 tons /ha`
