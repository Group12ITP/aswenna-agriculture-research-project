## Tile 05: Organic Fertilizer, Iron Toxic Soils, Salinity Management (`05_organic_irontoxic_salinity.png`)

### Organic fertilizer application
- `Use the disease free paddy straw from the previous season`
- `Use 1000kg/ac of leaf manure after the primary land preparation`
- `Use decay compost/cowdung after the secondary land preparation`
- `If apply poultry manure apply 1000 kg/ac`
- `After 2nd land preparation apply half burn paddy husk 250 kg/ac`
- `After appling organic fertilizers keep the paddy land in wet condition`

### Solution for iron toxic paddy lands
- `Dig deep drain around the paddy field`
- `Control the water flow in to the paddy field by small drain around paddy field`
- `After second land preparation fill the paddy field & drain the water`
- `Use additional Muriate of potash`
- `Apply dolamite`
- `Keep in soil dry condition`
- `Use resistance paddy varieties (Bw 267-3, Bw 361, Bw 363, Bw 364, Bw 367, Bw 372)`

### Solution to salinity problem
- `Bunds to prevent sea water mixing`
- `Land preparation with rain`
- `Improve drainage system`
- `Use organic fertilizer/half burn paddy husk`
- `Well land levelling`
- `Use seeding planting method`
- `Always flood the paddy field`
- `Use Resistence paddy varieties - (Bg 310, Bg 369, At 354)`
