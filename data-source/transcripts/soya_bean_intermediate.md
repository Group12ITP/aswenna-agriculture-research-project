## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_all.png`)

### Mosaic virus
- `Remove infected plants`
- `Crop rotation`
- `Control white fly`

### Purple seed stain
- `Use healthy seeds`
- `Seed treatment`
- `Apply recommended fungicides- during panicle initiation`

### Cercospora leaf spot
- `Use recommended spacing`
- `Field and crop sanitation`
- `Recommended fungicides`:
  - `Mancozeb 80% WG 20g/10 ltr`
  - `Propineb 70% WP 20/10 ltr`

### Pod borer
- `Collect and destroy Pod borers`
- `Remove and destroy crop residues`

### Bean fly
- `Early planting during Yala season (April - May)`
- `Seed treatment`: `Thiamethoxam 70% WS`
- `Apply fungicides when first pair of leaves appear after planting`
- `Recommended soil fungicides`:
  - `Diazinon 500g/EC/EW`
  - `Carbosulfan 200g/SC`

### Collar rot
- `Remove infected plant debris with soil`
- `Improve drainage`
- `Recommended soil fungicides`:
  - `Thiram 80% WP 70g/50 ltr/10m²`
  - `Thiophanate Methyl 70% WP`
  - `Thiram 30% WP 30g/50 ltr/10m²`

### Pod sucking bug
- `Recommended insecticides`:
  - `Carbaryl 85% WG 30gm/10 ltr`
  - `Carbaryl 48% SC 25/10 ltr`

### Anthracnose
- `Use healthy seeds`
- `Remove infected plants`
- `Recommended fungicides`:
  - `Manacozeb 80% WP 20g/10 ltr`
  - `Chlorothalonil 500g SC 30ml/10 ltr`

## Center Section: Seed Inoculation & Mechanization (`probe_lower_tables.png`)

### Seed Inoculation
- `Seed inoculation is necessary for Soyabean cultivation, where leguminous crops are not cultivated earlier`
- `1. Method I (for 50g-60g of seed)`:
  - `Heap seeds on ground`
  - `Apply two cups of water to get wet`
  - `Seed are needed to dissolve in 250g of Bacteria medium`
- `2. Method II (for 50g-60g of seed)`:
  - `Dissolve 250g of Bacteria medium with two cups of water to make it as a paste`
  - `Dissolve thoroughly with the seeds`
- `Important`:
  - `Plant inoculated seeds as soon as possible`
  - `Avoid direct sun light or drying out of seeds`
  - `Urea is not required for the basal dressing`

### Mechanization
- `Mechanization is important to obtain profitable cultivation of Soyabean`
- Pictured implements:
  - `Rotary seeder`
  - `Machinery for inter cultivation`
