## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_all.png`)

### 1. Thrips
- `Thiacloprid 24 g / l SC`: `10 ml /10 ltr`
- `Imidacloprid 20 g / l SL`: `10 ml /10 ltr`
- `Fipronil 50 g / l SC`: `10 ml /10 ltr`
- `Prothiophos 50 g / l EC`: `30 ml /10 ltr`

### 2. Anthracnose & Purple blotch
- `Pyraclostrobin 250 g/l EC`: `08 ml / 10 ltr`
- `Chlorothalonil 500 g / l SC`: `250 ml / 10 ltr`
- `Use of diseases free bulbs`
- `Maintain sanitation`
- `Crop Rotation`
- `Remove infected parts`

### 3. Damping-off
- `Captan 50 g / l WP`: `06 g`
- `Thiram 80 g / l SC`: `20 g`

### 4. Fungal bulb rot & Bacteria bulb rot
- **Seed Treatment**:
  - `Thiophanate-methyl 70 g / l WP`: `20 g / 10 ltr`
  - `Thiram 80 g / l WP`: `15 g /10 ltr`
- `Remove infected parts`
- `Improve drainage facilities`
- `Avoid irrigation from infected cultivations`

### 5. Big onion caterpillar (`Spodoptera litura`, `Spodoptera exigua`)
- `Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG`: `4 mg / 10 ltr`
- `Metaflumizone 24 g / l SC`: `25 ml / 10 ltr`

  - `Maha Season - Cultivate on ridges`

  - `Crop - 10cm x 10cm`
