## Tile 05: Pest Controlling Measures (`05_pest_control_measures.png`)

1. **Leaf spot & Rust**:
   - `Weed control`
   - `Crop rotation`
   - `Recommended fungicides`:
     - `Chlorothalonil 75% WP 20g/10 ltr`
     - `Mancozeb 80% WP 20g/10 ltr`
2. **Root rot & Bacterial wilt**:
   - `Removal of infected plant parts with soil`
   - `Improve drainage`
   - `Crop rotation`
   - `Expose soil to direct sunlight`
3. **Red case worms**:
   - `Collect and destroy- caterpillars and eggs`
4. **Army worms**:
   - `Collect and destroy pupae`
5. **Aphids**:
   - `Acts as a vector for viral diseases`
   - `Recommended fungicides` *(printed as fungicides)*:
     - `Thiamethoxam 25% WG 3g/10 ltr`
     - `Imidacloprid 70% WG 1.25g/10 ltr`
6. **Bud rot**:
   - `Use resistant varieties-Indi`
   - `Weed control`
   - `Control of Thrips`
   - `Remove infected plants`
   - `Planting cover crop - Maize`
7. **Scirtothrips dorsalis**:
   - `Recommended fungicides` *(printed as fungicides)*:
     - `Thiamethoxam 25% WG 5g/10 ltr`
     - `Imidacloprid 70% WG 1.25g/10 ltr`
